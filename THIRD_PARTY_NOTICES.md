## Planetary images

The visual tasks use 22 image files derived from NASA mission imagery.
The per-file source pages and credit lines are listed in
[`data/benchmark/assets/sources.json`](data/benchmark/assets/sources.json).
Credits include NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS, and
NASA/JPL-Caltech/Cornell University, as specified by each source.

Use the images with their respective credit lines. See the
[JPL Image Use Policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/)
and the individual source pages for terms and exceptions. The code license does
not relicense these images. No NASA, JPL, Caltech, or other institutional
endorsement is claimed.
